# Financial Engineering Study Guide

A practical, no-fluff study guide for people preparing for quant and financial-engineering interviews — and for self-study of the core toolkit: discounting, fixed income, derivatives, risk, and portfolio theory.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic in the left nav is a **standalone page you can read in ~10 minutes**.
- Every page follows the same shape: **why it matters**, **core concepts**, a **mental model**, **interview questions** with brief answer keys, and a short **watch list** of verified YouTube videos.
- Topics are ordered roughly from foundational to applied. You can read them in order, or jump straight to the topic you need.
- Interview badges tell you where to spend prep time: **🎯 Frequent**, **Occasional**, or **Background**.
- Nothing here requires a backend, market data, an API key, or a login — it's just reading.
